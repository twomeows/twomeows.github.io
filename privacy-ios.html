Privacy Policy — TwoMeows for iPhone and iPad 🔒

Effective date: 7 November 2026

TwoMeows is an app with bilingual audio stories for children aged 2 to 7. This policy applies to the iOS version of the app (iPhone and iPad) distributed through the App Store. The app is used by children, so we deliberately collect as little data as possible. Below is exactly what the app actually does.

In short

  • No ads. The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app never asks for permission to track.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • The child's profile (name, avatar, age), reading progress and stickers are stored only on the device.
  • An account is optional. Without one, the app works with an anonymous identifier.
  • You can delete the account and the associated data at any time, right in the app.

When the app downloads stories or connects to Firebase, Google's servers technically receive the device's IP address, as with any internet connection. We do not store it and do not use it to determine location.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and do not track users.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party marketing analytics; Google Analytics for Firebase is not included in the iOS app.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of children.
  • The child's name and age never leave the device.

Children

The app is designed for children. We follow the App Store Review Guidelines for apps for children, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including its provisions on minors.

An account can be created only by an adult (a parent or guardian), and only if they wish — the app works fully without it. The name parents enter for the child's profile never leaves the device: it is used only to address the child on screen. We recommend using a first name or a nickname rather than the child's full name.

Subscriptions are bought only through Apple's standard purchase window and must be confirmed by the owner of the Apple Account. If Family Sharing is set up, Apple's "Ask to Buy" and Screen Time settings apply.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase services — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage (story downloads) and Crashlytics — and Apple's App Store for subscriptions. Google processes the data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms and the Google Privacy Policy; payments are handled under the Apple Privacy Policy. These providers are bound by their terms to protect the data at least to the level described in this policy. The app uses no other third-party services.

Security

Data is transmitted only over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain, and access to server data is restricted by Firebase security rules.

How long data is kept

  • Data on the device — until you delete the child's profile, the account or the app.
  • Account and e-mail — until you delete the account. In the app, deletion is immediate; a request by e-mail is completed within 30 days.
  • Usage statistics — individual event records carry only the random identifier; after the account is deleted it can no longer be linked to anyone. They are used only in aggregated form.
  • Crash reports — kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.

Your rights

  • Delete the child's profile — in the app, in the profile settings.
  • Delete the account and data — in the app or by request, see below.
  • Get a copy of your data, correct it or restrict its processing — by e-mail to the address below.
  • Withdraw consent — delete the account or the app.
  • Complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app. Profile → Settings → Account settings → "Delete account" (the app interface is in Ukrainian: «Профіль → Налаштування → Налаштування акаунту → Видалити акаунт»). The action is irreversible and takes effect immediately. If you signed in a long time ago, the app will ask you to sign in again first.
  • By e-mail. Write to twomeoows@gmail.com from the address the account is registered with. We will complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted: the account, the e-mail address, the statistics profile linked to the account, children's profiles (name, avatar, age), reading progress, learned words, stickers and the subscription status stored on the device.

What remains: anonymous event records and daily totals without a name, e-mail or anything that identifies a person. Payment history remains with Apple under Apple's rules.

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device but does not delete the account — use one of the ways above. Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: manage or cancel it in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will appear on this page and the date at the top will change. We will announce significant changes in the app.

Contact
